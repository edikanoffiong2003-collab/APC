Senator Allwell Onyesoh Receives Friends of O.K. Chinda, Reaffirms Commitment to APC Unity

By Peter Oriri (Special Adviser to the Senator, Publicity & Communications) • November 2, 2026 • Port Harcourt, Rivers State

The chairman, Senate committee on works, Distinguished Senator (Chief) Allwell Heacho Onyesoh, DSSRS, JP, FIBAKM, representing Rivers East Senatorial District, on Monday, November 2, 2026, received members of the Friends of O.K. Chinda Movement on a courtesy and solidarity visit at his PortHarcourt residence.

Fig 1. Distinguished Senator Allwell Onyesoh receiving members of the Friends of O.K. Chinda Movement at his Port Harcourt residence.

The delegation, led by its Coordinator, Comrade Austin Amadi, expressed support for Senator Onyesoh's re-election bid, commending his performance, quality representation and commitment to the development of Rivers East.

He also reaffirmed the group's support for all Rainbow Coalition candidates ahead of the 2027 general elections.

“With unity of purpose and collective commitment, I am confident that the APC will record a resounding victory in Rivers State come 2027.”

Responding, Senator Onyesoh appreciated the group for the visit and show of solidarity. He commended their commitment to the All Progressives Congress (APC) and urged them to sustain their support for the Honourable Minister of the Federal Capital Territory and Leader of the Rainbow Coalition, Chief Nyesom Wike.

Fig 2. Address by Comrade Austin Amadi.
Fig 3. Executive delegation members.

“Let me appreciate you all for this show of love and solidarity. I commend your commitment to our great party, the APC, and urge you to continue to align with and support the Honourable Minister of the Federal Capital Territory and Leader of the Rainbow Coalition, Chief Nyesom Wike, who means well for Rivers people and remains committed to the peace, progress and political stability of our state. With unity of purpose and collective commitment, I am confident that the APC will record a resounding victory in Rivers State come 2027,” He said.

The visit reaffirmed the importance of unity, solidarity and collective commitment among party stakeholders as the APC continues to consolidate its support base ahead of the 2027 general elections in Rivers State.

Fig 4. Group photograph with Senator Onyesoh.
Fig 5. APC stakeholders in attendance.

OFFICIAL PRESS STATEMENT • APC RIVERS EAST

Etche, Omuma Legislative Councils Commend Senator Onyesoh, Reaffirm Support

By Peter Oriri (Special Adviser to the Senator, Publicity & Communications) • October 5, 2026 • Port Harcourt, Rivers State

The Etche 10th and Omuma 7th Legislative Councils have commended the Senator representing Rivers East Senatorial District and Chairman of the Senate Committee on Works, Distinguished Senator (Chief) Allwell Heacho Onyesoh, DSSRS, JP, FIBAKM, for his effective representation at the 10th National Assembly and affirmed their support for his reelection for a second term in 2027.

Fig 1. Distinguished Senator Onyesoh welcoming members of the Etche and Omuma Legislative Councils.

The position was expressed during a courtesy visit by members of the two Legislative Councils at his Port Harcourt Residence, where they discussed issues concerning the senatorial district, his 2027 reelection bid and their shared commitment to the Rainbow Coalition.

Leader of the Omuma 7th Legislative Assembly, Rt. Hon. Ruth Chika Nweke, said the councillors appreciated Senator Onyesoh’s representation and expressed their readiness to support his return to the Senate, adding that they would mobilise grassroots support ahead of 2027.

“Councillors will be considered for appointment as Ward Coordinators, as their grassroots experience is valuable to the campaign.”

Similarly, the Leader of the Etche 10th Legislative Assembly, Rt. Hon. Marshal Nwankwo, commended Senator Onyesoh for his outstanding representation and reaffirmed the absolute commitment of Etche councillors to his reelection.

Fig 2. Legislative leaders addressing the meeting during the visit.

Responding, Senator Onyesoh thanked the legislative leaders and their colleagues for the visit. He assured them that his 2027 campaign would be broad-based and inclusive, with Councillors playing important leadership roles in the structure.

The Senator disclosed that Councillors would be considered for appointment as Ward Coordinators, noting that their grassroots experience and close relationship with the people would be valuable assets to the campaign machinery.

Fig 3. Cross section of councillors.
Fig 4. Group photograph with Senator Onyesoh.

The visit concluded with a unified resolve to strengthen grassroots participation across Rivers East Senatorial District.

OFFICIAL PRESS STATEMENT • APC RIVERS EAST

Senator Onyesoh, Kano Governor, Goronyo & Others Commission 118km Kaduna–Zaria–Kano Road

By Peter Oriri (Special Adviser to the Senator, Publicity & Communications) • October 30, 2026 • Abuja / Kano State

The Chairman, Senate Committee on Works and Senator representing Rivers East Senatorial District, Distinguished Senator (Chief) Allwell Heacho Onyesoh, DSSRS, JP, FIBAKM, today joined the Executive Governor of Kano State, His Excellency, Abba Kabir Yusuf; the Honourable Minister of State for Works, Bello Muhammad Goronyo Esq; members of the National Assembly and other dignitaries at the commissioning of a completed 30.58-kilometre section of the 118-kilometre Kaduna–Zaria–Kano Road project in Kano State.

Fig 1. Senator Onyesoh with Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf and Minister Bello Muhammad Goronyo at the commissioning site.

The road is a major transport corridor linking Kaduna, Zaria and Kano, three important commercial and population centres in Northern Nigeria. Its rehabilitation and expansion are expected to improve connectivity, reduce travel time and vehicle operating costs, enhance road safety and facilitate the movement of agricultural produce, goods, services and passengers.

The project also has significant economic advantages for businesses, farmers, transport operators and communities along the corridor. By improving access between these major centres, the road is expected to support trade, strengthen regional commerce and create better opportunities for economic activities across Northern Nigeria.

“Through investments in strategic road projects, the administration is working to strengthen transportation networks and support economic activities nationwide.”

The commissioning forms part of the Federal Government’s broader infrastructure development programme under President Bola Ahmed Tinubu. Through investments in strategic road projects, the administration is working to strengthen transportation networks, improve access to markets and support economic activities across different regions of the country.

Fig 2. A section of the newly rehabilitated 118km transport corridor.

Senator Onyesoh’s participation in the commissioning reflects his legislative oversight responsibilities as Chairman of the Senate Committee on Works and his engagement with developments in the country’s road infrastructure sector.

Stakeholders have continued to praise the collaborative efforts between the legislative arm and the executive in delivering transformative infrastructure that directly impacts the daily lives of citizens and boosts national productivity.

Fig 3. Dignitaries inspecting the project.
Fig 4. Ribbon-cutting ceremony.

The event concluded with goodwill messages from national leaders and representatives of transport unions along the corridor.

Senator Allwell Onyesoh Offers Full Scholarship to Late Prof. Otamiri’s Son, Donates ₦2m for Burial Rites

By Peter O. Oriri (Special Adviser to the Senator, Publicity & Communications) • September 12, 2026 • Port Harcourt, Rivers State

The Senator representing Rivers East Senatorial District and Chairman, Senate Committee on Works, Distinguished Senator (Chief) Allwell Heacho Onyesoh, DSSRS, has demonstrated a remarkable act of compassion and commitment to human-capital development by offering a comprehensive scholarship to Mr. David Sam Otamiri, the son of his late bosom friend and brother, Professor Sam Ababa Otamiri who died in July, 2026.

Fig 1. Distinguished Senator Allwell Onyesoh announcing the full scholarship intervention.

David, a 200-level student of Computer Engineering at Rivers State University, will benefit from the scholarship throughout the remainder of his academic programme. The intervention will cover the balance of his studies, providing him with the opportunity to pursue and complete his degree without financial constraints.

Senator Onyesoh made the scholarship offer at a meeting of the Central Burial Committee set up to coordinate the burial arrangements of the late Professor Otamiri, which is chaired by former Vice Chancellor of Ignatius Ajuru University of Education, Professor Ozo Mekuri Ndimele.

“A gesture that speaks to the value of genuine friendship and the responsibility to stand with one another in moments of grief and difficulty.”

In addition to taking responsibility for the completion of the young man’s education, Senator Onyesoh donated the sum of ₦2,000,000 towards the burial arrangements, easing the responsibilities associated with the burial at a difficult time for the family.

Fig 2. Members of the Central Burial Committee during the briefing.

The intervention reflects Senator Onyesoh’s consistent emphasis on human-capital development and the importance of empowering young people through education, offering both immediate relief and enduring hope.

At a time when the Otamiri family is confronted with the pain of losing a loved one, this act highlights the true essence of brotherhood and community solidarity.

Fig 3. David Sam Otamiri.
Fig 4. Committee members and stakeholders.

May the soul of Professor Sam Ababa Otamiri rest in perfect peace.
