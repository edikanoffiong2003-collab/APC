RIVERS EAST SENATORIAL DISTRICT GAZETTE

The Human Capital Flag Stays Waving

By Senator Allwell Onyesoh Press Team • October 2026 • Port Harcourt, Rivers State

Today, the good people of Rivers East Senatorial District have once again entrusted me with their confidence as I formally received the flag of the All Progressives Congress (APC) as its candidate for the January 2027 senatorial election.

Fig 1. Senator Allwell Onyesoh receiving the APC Senatorial Flag.

I express my profound appreciation to the people of Rivers East for their continued support and trust; the Honourable Minister of the Federal Capital Territory (FCT) and General Overseer of the Rainbow Coalition, His Excellency, Chief Barr. Ezenwo Nyesom Wike; the APC, under the progressive leadership of President Bola Ahmed Tinubu; and all well-meaning Rivers men and women who stood with us today.

"The Rainbow Coalition represents a commitment to peaceful coexistence, shared prosperity and issue-based politics."

Today marks another defining moment in Rivers politics. The Rainbow Coalition, bringing together political parties and stakeholders across the state, represents a commitment to peaceful coexistence, shared prosperity and issue-based politics. It reflects the aspirations of ordinary Rivers people who want to participate meaningfully in governance. I commend Chief Nyesom Wike for his vision, commitment and leadership in advancing this political movement.

Fig 2. Stakeholders gathered during the Rainbow Coalition alignment.

Over the last three years, I have remained steadfast in my belief that sustainable development begins with investment in people. This conviction has guided our efforts to create employment opportunities for young people across Rivers East, support families and positively impact lives. Our human capital philosophy remains the foundation of my representation.

As I seek a second term, my commitment is to build on these achievements, consolidate the progress made and deliver more tangible benefits to our people. I return to the people of Rivers East with a record of service and a renewed determination to do more for our senatorial district.

Fig 3. Senator Onyesoh interacting with community delegates.

I urge all my supporters, well-wishers and admirers to extend the same support to other candidates of the Rainbow Coalition, particularly His Excellency, President Bola Ahmed Tinubu, and the incoming Governor of Rivers State, Rt. Hon. O. K. Chinda.

Together, we can build a senatorial district, a state and a nation where young people can pursue their aspirations with confidence and have access to the opportunities they need to succeed.

The human capital flag will continue to wave, and all will be well with Rivers East!
God bless Rivers East Senatorial District!
God bless Rivers State!!
God bless the Federal Republic of Nigeria!!!

PRESS STATEMENT • LEGISLATIVE DISPATCH

Etche, Omuma Legislative Councils Commend Senator Allwell Onyesoh, Reaffirm Support for Second Term Bid

By Peter Oriri (Special Adviser, Publicity & Communications) • 5th October, 2026

The Etche 10th and Omuma 7th Legislative Councils have commended the Senator representing Rivers East Senatorial District and Chairman of the Senate Committee on Works, Distinguished Senator (Chief) Allwell Heacho Onyesoh, DSSRS, JP, FIBAKM, for his effective representation at the 10th National Assembly and affirmed their support for his reelection for a second term in 2027.

Fig 1. Councillors from Etche & Omuma Legislative Councils during their courtesy visit.

The position was expressed during a courtesy visit by members of the two Legislative Councils at his Port Harcourt Residence, where they discussed issues concerning the senatorial district, his 2027 reelection bid and their shared commitment to the Rainbow Coalition.

"Councillors would be considered for appointment as Ward Coordinators, noting their grassroots experience."

Leader of the Omuma 7th Legislative Assembly, Rt. Hon. Ruth Chika Nweke, said the councillors appreciated Senator Onyesoh’s representation and expressed their readiness to support his return to the Senate.

She added that the councillors would work with stakeholders at the grassroots to mobilise support for his second-term bid ahead of the 2027 elections.

Similarly, the Leader of the Etche 10th Legislative Assembly, Rt. Hon. Marshal Nwankwo, commended Senator Onyesoh for his representation and reaffirmed the commitment of the Etche councillors to his reelection.

He said the councillors remained committed to the Rainbow Coalition and would support efforts aimed at strengthening unity and grassroots participation across Rivers East Senatorial District.

Fig 2. Senator Allwell Onyesoh flanked by Rt. Hon. Marshal Nwankwo and Rt. Hon. Ruth Chika Nweke.

Responding, Senator Onyesoh thanked the legislative leaders and their colleagues for the visit and expression of support. He assured them that his 2027 campaign would be broad-based and inclusive, with Councillors expected to play important roles in the campaign structure.

The Senator disclosed that Councillors would be considered for appointment as Ward Coordinators, noting that their grassroots experience and close relationship with the people would be valuable to the campaign.

Signed: Peter Oriri (Special Adviser to the Senator, Publicity & Communications)

PRESS STATEMENT • HUMAN CAPITAL DISPATCH

Senator Allwell Onyesoh Reaffirms Commitment to Human Capital Development as Employment Beneficiaries Pay Appreciation Visit

By Senator Allwell Onyesoh Media Team • Saturday, October 3, 2026

Beneficiaries of employment opportunities facilitated through the intervention of the Senator representing Rivers East Senatorial District, Distinguished Senator (Chief) Allwell Onyesoh, DSSRS, JP, FIBAKM, have paid an appreciation visit to the Senator at his residence, acknowledging his efforts towards improving the lives and livelihoods of constituents.

Fig 1. Senator Allwell Onyesoh with employment beneficiaries.

The delegation, led by Engr. Chima TOS Anaele, comprised some of the beneficiaries of the Senator’s employment facilitation initiative. They expressed appreciation for the opportunities, which they said had provided them with a means of livelihood, professional growth and greater economic stability.

Over 200 people across the Rivers East Senatorial District have so far benefited from Senator Onyesoh’s human capital development drive, particularly through employment opportunities and other interventions aimed at creating pathways for constituents to become economically productive.

Fig 2. Beneficiaries presenting support materials.
"Public office should be used to create opportunities that enable people to improve their lives and contribute meaningfully to society."

Speaking on behalf of the delegation, Engr. Anaele acknowledged the impact of the opportunities facilitated by the Senator, noting that the beneficiaries considered the visit necessary to personally express their appreciation for the support that had positively affected their careers and family livelihoods.

The beneficiaries also presented materials in support of Senator Onyesoh’s re-election bid and expressed their continued support as the nation approaches another electoral cycle.

Fig 3. Senator Onyesoh addressing the delegation.

Receiving the delegation, Senator Onyesoh thanked them for the visit and appreciated their recognition of the importance of the employment opportunities. He said his commitment to human capital development remains firm, stressing that public office should be used to create opportunities that enable people to improve their lives and contribute meaningfully to society.

The Senator further assured the delegation that he would continue to support initiatives that promote employment, empowerment and human development across Rivers East, while remaining focused on the welfare and progress of his constituents.

The visit brought into focus the practical impact of employment facilitation as a form of public service. For the beneficiaries, the opportunities have gone beyond securing jobs to providing income, building professional confidence and enabling them to support their families and contribute to their communities.
