Privacy Policy

Effective Date: Campaign Season 2027

The Senator Allwell Onyesoh APC 2027 Re-election Campaign ("we", "our", or "the Campaign") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of our supporters, agents, and volunteers across Rivers East Senatorial District in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA).

1. Information We Collect

We collect voluntary information provided through our Grassroots Mobilization Hub and authentication forms, including:

  • Full Name and Contact Information (Phone / WhatsApp number)
  • Local Government Area (LGA) and Ward in Rivers East
  • Voter registration status / PVC registration indicators
  • Agent volunteer recruitment commitments and 20-voter roster logs

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly processed for legitimate campaign coordination purposes:

  • Verifying grassroots supporter counts and polling unit readiness
  • Sending official election updates and mobilization alerts via WhatsApp/SMS
  • Enabling field agents to maintain local 20-voter rosters
  • Organizing ward-level campaign events and agent training

3. Data Protection & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to commercial third parties. Data is restricted strictly to authorized APC Rivers East campaign coordinators and stored client-side for individual field agent operational efficiency.

4. Your Data Rights

You reserve the right to request access to your personal details, request updates to inaccurate voter entries, or request the removal of your information from our mobilization database at any time by contacting campaign headquarters.
