SENATOR ALLWELL ONYESOH — PROJECTS & GALLERY

Rivers East Senatorial District — 10th National Assembly

1. Empowerment Schemes & Human Capital

  • Employment Facilitation (200+ Beneficiaries): Facilitated direct employment opportunities across federal agencies (including NCAA) and key public institutions for over 200 constituents across Rivers East.
  • Law School "Call to Bar" Educational Grant (₦23.85 Million): Disbursed ₦150,000 cash grants to 159 law graduates from Rivers East to cover Call to Bar fees, wigs, gowns, and logistical expenses.
  • Postgraduate Legal Scholarships: Awarded full postgraduate law (LL.M.) scholarships to two young lawyers—Azunda Blessing and Fortunatus Chisom Njoku.
  • Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) Training (₦6.7 Million): Sponsored specialized ADR certification and capacity-building training for young legal professionals.
  • Vulnerable Constituents Support: Sustains vocational training, skill acquisition, and financial support through his constituency office.

2. Constituency Outreach & Grassroots

  • Open-Door Grassroots Dialogue: Operates an accessible constituency headquarters in Port Harcourt, hosting regular interactive sessions with community leaders, youth groups, and women associations.

  • Ward-Level Structural Inclusion: Integrating local councillors into his outreach structure, appointing experienced leaders as Ward Coordinators to deliver democratic dividends across all wards.

3. Legislative Record & Oversight

  • Key Legislation Sponsored: Sponsored the Federal Character Commission Act (Amendment) Bill, 2025, to enforce transparency and equity in recruitment across Nigeria.
  • Senate Committee Leadership: Appointed as Chairman of the Senate Committee on Works, leading national oversight on federal road infrastructure and budget execution.
  • Institutional Oversight: Performs legislative oversight functions across statutory bodies including NNPCL, NUPRC, and NAFDAC.

4. Grassroots Endorsements & 2027 Movement

  • Town Hall Consultations with FCT Minister Nyesom Wike: Senator Onyesoh hosted high-level town hall meetings and grassroots stakeholder engagements alongside the Minister of the Federal Capital Territory (FCT), Chief (Barr.) Nyesom Ezenwo Wike, in Etche Council Secretariat (Okehi) and across Rivers East, reinforcing party cohesion, grassroots synergy, and collective political direction under the Rainbow Coalition.
  • APC Platform Re-Election Bid: Reaffirming his commitment to continuous governance, Senator Onyesoh officially declared his candidacy for a second term in the Senate under the platform of the All Progressives Congress (APC).
  • District-Wide Endorsements: His re-election movement has garnered firm backing and endorsements from local legislative assemblies (including the Etche 10th and Omuma 7th Legislative Councils), traditional councils, youth groups, and key political blocs across all 8 LGAs of Rivers East.
