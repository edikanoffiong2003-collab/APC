Senator Allwell Onyesoh Receives Friends of O.K. Chinda, Reaffirms Commitment to APC Unity

By Peter Oriri (Special Adviser to the Senator, Publicity & Communications) • November 2, 2026 • Port Harcourt, Rivers State

The chairman, Senate committee on works, Distinguished Senator (Chief) Allwell Heacho Onyesoh, DSSRS, JP, FIBAKM, representing Rivers East Senatorial District, on Monday, November 2, 2026, received members of the Friends of O.K. Chinda Movement on a courtesy and solidarity visit at his PortHarcourt residence.

Fig 1. Distinguished Senator Allwell Onyesoh receiving members of the Friends of O.K. Chinda Movement at his Port Harcourt residence.

The delegation, led by its Coordinator, Comrade Austin Amadi, expressed support for Senator Onyesoh's re-election bid, commending his performance, quality representation and commitment to the development of Rivers East.

He also reaffirmed the group's support for all Rainbow Coalition candidates ahead of the 2027 general elections.

“With unity of purpose and collective commitment, I am confident that the APC will record a resounding victory in Rivers State come 2027.”

Responding, Senator Onyesoh appreciated the group for the visit and show of solidarity. He commended their commitment to the All Progressives Congress (APC) and urged them to sustain their support for the Honourable Minister of the Federal Capital Territory and Leader of the Rainbow Coalition, Chief Nyesom Wike.

Fig 2. Address by Comrade Austin Amadi.
Fig 3. Executive delegation members.

“Let me appreciate you all for this show of love and solidarity. I commend your commitment to our great party, the APC, and urge you to continue to align with and support the Honourable Minister of the Federal Capital Territory and Leader of the Rainbow Coalition, Chief Nyesom Wike, who means well for Rivers people and remains committed to the peace, progress and political stability of our state. With unity of purpose and collective commitment, I am confident that the APC will record a resounding victory in Rivers State come 2027,” He said.

The visit reaffirmed the importance of unity, solidarity and collective commitment among party stakeholders as the APC continues to consolidate its support base ahead of the 2027 general elections in Rivers State.
